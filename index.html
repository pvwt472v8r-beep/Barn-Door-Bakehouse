About Us

Welcome to The Barn Door Bakehouse, a cozy little bake stand where every loaf, bagel, and cookie is made from scratch with love. We're a family-run home bakery, and our sourdough starter is the heart of almost everything we make.

Our menu changes with the seasons, so there's always something new to look forward to. Stop by the stand, scan the QR code, and pick up something fresh and warm.

Thank you for supporting our small business. We can't wait to share our bakes with you!
